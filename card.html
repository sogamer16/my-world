Tap: an NFC card or sticker

  1. Buy a blank NTAG215 or NTAG216 card or sticker. A white PVC card is the same size as the printed card below, so the print can go straight on top.
  2. Install the free NFC Tools app (iPhone or Android). Choose Write → Add a record → URL.
  3. Enter https://sogamer16.github.io/my-world/, press Write, and hold the tag to the top of the phone.
  4. Test it with another phone. Once it works, use Other → Lock tag so nobody can overwrite it. Locking is permanent.

Phones read the tag with no app: the screen only has to be on and unlocked. A sticker on the back of a phone case works too, as long as the case is not metal.

Scan: a printed card

  1. Pick "My world" or "My CV" above, then press Print cards.
  2. Print at 100% scale (not "fit to page") on A4 card stock. Each sheet gives ten cards, 85.6 × 54 mm, cut on the dashed lines.

No printer nearby? This page on your phone is the card: hold the code up and let them scan it.
